Job Description: Hindi Evaluator & Remote Candidate

Please read the entire job requirements carefully before unlocking the application form.

  • Role: Hindi Language Specialist – AI Conversation.
  • Job Type: Online / Remote Work.
  • Work Description: Participate in Hindi conversations with an AI system to help train and improve AI language models.
  • Payment: Hourly-based payment in USD ($). Candidates will be paid for eligible hours spent participating in conversations.
  • Eligibility: Students, Freshers, Housewives, Job Seekers, and Experienced Candidates are welcome.
  • Language Requirements: Good Hindi speaking and conversational skills are required.
  • Location: Applicants must be residing in India.
  • Technical Requirements: An active smartphone and a stable, high-speed internet connection are required.
  • Work Environment: Access to a quiet room with proper lighting and minimal background noise.
  • Documentation: Valid Aadhaar Card or PAN Card is mandatory for identity verification.
  • Evaluation Process: A brief Hindi language screening will be conducted through a telephonic or video interaction after receiving the application.
